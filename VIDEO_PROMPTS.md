# AI Video Prompts

Generate these with Sora, Veo, Kling, Runway or a similar tool, export them as **MP4 (H.264), muted, under 4 MB each**, and save them to `public/videos/` with the exact file names below. Until a file exists, its slot shows an animated gradient.

Style for every clip follows the **Pandora Templates brand**: warm, organised and quietly elegant. Use soft natural window light on cream linen, with plum (#3B1F4A), marigold/gold ribbon (#F2A93B), peacock teal (#1F7A74) and coral rose (#E0675A) accents, and slow, smooth camera motion. Think premium gift-unboxing photography. The clips should loop well and have no text (the site adds its own).

## Prompts

**hero.mp4**
> A plum gift box tied with a gold satin ribbon sits on cream linen in warm morning window light. The lid gently lifts open and a soft golden sparkle rises out, revealing a laptop screen glowing with a tidy wedding planner spreadsheet. Shallow depth of field, slow seamless push-in loop.

**tab-budget.mp4**
> Close-up of hands tapping a phone calculator as gold coins and rupee notes fly neatly into labelled jars (Venue, Catering, Decor) on a bright yellow table. Satisfying, organised, playful motion, top-down shot.

**tab-guests.mp4**
> Top-down shot of colourful wedding invitation cards sliding into neat rows on a pink table, each one getting a green tick sticker. Cheerful, tidy, satisfying animation.

**tab-vendors.mp4**
> A playful flat-lay of wedding vendor items arriving one by one: a camera, marigold garlands, a DJ headphone, a makeup brush and a biryani handi, each snapping into a neat grid. Cream linen background, warm light, gentle stop-motion feel.

**tab-payments.mp4**
> A cute wall calendar with dates being circled in pink marker, coins dropping into a piggy bank wearing a tiny wedding turban, and a green "PAID" stamp landing. Bright, playful, stop-motion style.

**tab-dashboard.mp4**
> Colourful 3D bar charts and a donut chart rising out of a laptop screen like a pop-up book, with confetti bursting. Plum, marigold and teal palette, cream background, satisfying motion.

**final-cta.mp4**
> Joyful Indian wedding sangeet dance floor at night, couple and friends dancing under warm fairy lights with flying marigold petals, shallow depth of field, slow motion, warm plum and gold tones. Seamless loop.

Tip: also export the first frame of each clip as a JPG poster image and pass it as the `poster` prop to `VideoSlot`, so something shows instantly on slow mobile connections.
