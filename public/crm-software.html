Questions, answered

Something else? Write to us.

Do I need to connect my Facebook account?

No. Download your leads from Meta Leads Center and upload the file, or send them to a Google Sheet and connect that sheet. Both take a few minutes to set up.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. LeadFlow runs in the browser on any phone or computer, and you can install it on your home screen to get alerts for new leads and due follow-ups.

Can my team see each other's leads?

Callers only see the leads given to them. Admins see every lead, every call and the team report.

What happens when my trial ends?

You can still log in and read all your data, but changes are paused until you pick a plan. Nothing is ever deleted, and you can export everything to CSV at any time.

Is my data safe from other companies?

Yes. Every company has its own separate workspace, and the system is tested to make sure one company can never see or change another company's leads.

Can I import leads I already have?

Yes. Import any CSV, match your columns once, and LeadFlow skips duplicates by phone and email. If something looks wrong, undo the whole import in one click.
