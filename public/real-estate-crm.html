Questions, answered

Something else? Write to us.

Can I add a whole tower of units at once?

Yes. Add a range (for example B-101 to B-1204), or import a CSV with unit number, type, area, floor and price. Every unit gets its own ID that never changes.

How do unit holds work?

Any team member can hold an available unit for a buyer. The hold lasts 48 hours by default (you can change it per project) and frees itself when it expires. Only the person who placed it, or an admin, can release it early.

What do channel partners see?

Partners get their own login. They register their buyers and see only their own buyers, never your other leads.

Do the project pages show my prices to everyone?

Unit and project pages show what you choose to share. Booked or sold units say "No longer available" with no price, and buyer and hold details are never shown.

What happens when my trial ends?

You can still log in and read all your data, but changes are paused until you pick a plan in the app (Menu → Plan & billing). It switches on as soon as you pay. Nothing is ever deleted, and you can export everything at any time.

Do I need to connect my Facebook account?

No. Download your leads from Meta Leads Center and upload the file, or send them to a Google Sheet and connect that sheet.
